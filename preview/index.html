01 / The through-line

Complex systems.
Human stakes.

My route into research began with responsibility for the way things work.

Leading real estate and transportation operations meant working where records, rules, technical systems, and human judgment meet. Building the infrastructure behind those operations made the questions sharper: What counts as evidence? Who can correct a record? When should a decision be reconsidered?

Today, I bring that experience into interdisciplinary research on human–AI judgment, information, and institutional answerability.

03 / Experience with direction

A practical foundation.
An expanding horizon.

I have led distributed teams, operated across regulatory environments, and learned to build the digital systems those teams depend on. Returning to scholarship brought a new purpose to that preparation: turning difficult practical questions into disciplined, testable inquiry.

I’m pursuing graduate study in Interdisciplinary Studies at the University of North Texas, connecting ethical technology, civic leadership, and human-centered innovation.

04 / What the work is for

Advance knowledge.
Make it useful.

The next step is a research program that connects rigorous inquiry with public understanding.

I’m developing a path toward accessible research materials, reproducible methods, and opportunities for students who bring practical experience into scholarship. Through DG Humana Institute, I’m building a setting for dialogue across academic, civic, nonprofit, and professional communities.
