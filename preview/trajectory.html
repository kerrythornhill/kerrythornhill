The questions
grew from the work.

I came to research after years of responsibility for operational decisions: records had to be reliable, rules had to be interpreted, teams had to coordinate, and systems had to work under real constraints.

That experience made me attentive to the distance between a process that runs smoothly and a decision that can withstand scrutiny. Learning to build the infrastructure brought the issue into sharper focus. Every permission, data source, and workflow shapes what people can know and do.

Interdisciplinary study gives me the space to examine those relationships more carefully. I draw on the humanities to question concepts and assumptions, on systems practice to trace operational choices, and on emerging empirical training to make the questions testable.

Experience / A cumulative foundation

From responsibility
to inquiry.

2012–presentReal estate brokerage

Leading people through consequential decisions.

My real estate experience spans commercial and residential property, multifamily housing, land, and farm and ranch transactions. I have also participated in mixed-use development projects and coordinated with local, county, and state authorities.

Leading a brokerage and the work of more than fifty agents required financial accountability, due diligence, negotiation, and coordination among people with different expertise. That work developed my ability to organize complex evidence, communicate uncertainty, and trace how records and requirements shape consequential decisions.

2020–2024Transportation operations

Solving problems across a distributed system.

As owner and operations director of Ave Maria Transport, I managed transportation operations across forty-eight states. The work involved routing, maintenance documentation, safety audits, contracts, and coordination with local, county, state, and federal authorities.

Operating across jurisdictions demanded persistence, systems thinking, and close attention to the relationship between formal requirements and their practical application.

Across these rolesDigital infrastructure

Building the systems behind the work.

Alongside these roles, I built and connected the digital systems used to coordinate records, communications, and operational workflows. Work with cloud infrastructure, data integrations, permissions, and automation made the movement and control of information a practical concern before it became a research question.
